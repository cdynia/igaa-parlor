How to play

Junior & Deluxe

The goal

Hold a 3-card appointment — one season, one day, one time, all the same color — then declare it.

Your turn

  • Draw from the main deck or the discard pile, then discard one card.
  • Drawing or holding a MAIL card triggers MAIL CALL: you replenish and flip a Good News / Bad News card, which either makes you lose a card or lets you ask an opponent for one. These resolve automatically and end your turn.
  • If an ASK FOR trade completes your appointment, you may declare it right away.

Deluxe

Each appointment wins the round and claims a matching Appointment card (color + weekday/weekend + AM/PM; NOON counts as either). Everything reshuffles and a new round begins. When every Appointment card is claimed, the player with the most wins.
