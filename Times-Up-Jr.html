Times Up Jr

Flip the months one at a time and complete a season's three months to score — but the hidden Noon card is somewhere in the grid. Reveal it first for 5, leave it for last for 4, or get caught in between.

Players
Rounds

Setup mirrors the box: twelve month cards with one secretly swapped for Noon, laid in a 3×4 grid. That means one season is always missing a month — it can never be completed.

How to play

Race the Noon card

Your turn

  • Flip face-down cards one at a time by clicking them.
  • Reveal all three months of a season to score that set (1 point).
  • Noon on your very first flip: 5 points, turn ends.
  • Noon after some flips: turn ends — you keep the sets you already scored.
  • Flip all eleven months and leave Noon for last: 4 points on top of your sets.

The catch

One month was swapped out for Noon, so one season only has two of its months on the table — that season can never be completed. Push your luck, or play it safe.

Winning

Play the chosen number of rounds. Add every round's points; the highest total wins.
