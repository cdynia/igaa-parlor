Season Wheel

Each turn you're dealt 12 cards. Build the best-scoring combos you can: appointments, bookends, sequences, Noons. Missing one card? Spin the Season Wheel and let the color finish it.

Players
Seats: name · who plays · birth month (for the Birthday Appointment)
Game length

Pass-and-play friendly: every turn is its own 12-card hand, so players can share one screen. Computer seats build and spin on their own.

How to play

Your turn

  • You're dealt 12 cards from the main deck.
  • Tap cards (or drag them into the Combo builder). When your picks make a combo, tap it to add it to This hand. Suggest best builds the highest-scoring plan for you.
  • You may make more than one combo of the same type.
  • Press Score & spin. Complete combos score right away. Combos marked -S may use the spinner to supply the missing card: spin the right color and you score, miss and that combo scores nothing.

Using a card twice

A card normally counts in one combo. The exceptions are Four Seasons (4S), 3 Months in a Season (3M) and Noons (3N/4N). A card in one of those sets can also be used in one other combo. Example: the September card in your Original Appointment can also help make 3 Months in a Season.

Bonus cards

Completing a 4-card combo earns a bonus card. That means Four Seasons (dealt or spun), all Four Noons, or winning the 3-Noon gamble. The bonus card joins your hand, and you can build more combos with it.

Winning

Play a single hand, or keep going round after round until someone reaches the game score. The round is always finished so everyone gets the same number of turns. Most points wins.

Digital edition notes

  • On Anne's sheets, "TA" means both Today's Appointment and 3 AM Times. Here, TA = Today's Appointment and the time combos are AM and PM.
  • The deck is reshuffled each round. If it runs low, the hands already scored that round are shuffled back in.
  • A card shared by 4S, 3M, or a Noon set can join one other combo. Two sets of the same shared type still need different cards.
  • "Today" uses this device's date. The day card is the day of the week.
