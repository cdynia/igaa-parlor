Questions from parents

Can my child play alone?

Yes. Most games have friendly computer players, so a child can play solo. Times Up and Season Wheel are also great for passing one device around the table.

Do we need an account, or is it free?

It's free and there's nothing to sign up for. No accounts and no ads.

Which game should we start with?

Ages 3–5: Level 1 Seasons. Ages 6 and up: Times Up, then Appointment. When those feel easy, move up to the games marked Advanced or the Ages 9+ group.

What do the Easy, Playful, and Advanced tags mean?

They show how much thinking a game takes. Easy is quick and simple, Playful adds a few choices, and Advanced has more strategy and scoring.

Does it work on a phone or tablet? Offline?

Yes to both. It works on phones, tablets, and computers. After your first visit you can play offline. To keep it handy, choose Add to Home Screen from your browser's menu.

Can we leave in the middle of a game?

Tap ⌂ Parlor at the top of any game. It asks before quitting so nobody loses a game by accident. Games aren't saved once you leave.
