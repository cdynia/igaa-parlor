How to play · Game #242

Ducks on the Lake

Object

Help your duck match its Time cards to the board so it can reach the lake. When it does, proudly declare "It's time for my duck to go for a swim!" The player with the most swims (points) wins.

The board

Seasons sit in the corners: Spring (Yellow), Summer (Green), Fall (Orange) and Winter (Blue). Yellow, Green and Orange have specific time spaces plus general AM/PM spaces. Blue has only AM and PM spaces. Purple spaces are AnyTime spaces for NOON cards.

Your turn — draw one Time card

  • Specific match (color and time): move there and keep the card as a PASS card. Three PASS cards = a swim, 3 points.
  • General AM/PM match: move to that color's AM or PM space and discard the card.
  • Blue cards match only AM or PM. Hold them until you have one AM and one PM — swim, 2 points.
  • Docks — Yellow NOON, Orange 9 AM and Green 1 PM — swim right away, 4 points.
  • NOON cards: Yellow NOON is a Dock. The Blue NOON is wild (Blue pair, escaping a corner, or a NOON for the AnyTime swim). Two non-Yellow NOONs on two turns in a row send your duck to the Blue AnyTime Swim dock — 5 points.
  • Bumping: land on another duck and it goes to the Season corner of your card's color. It waits there until it draws that color or any NOON, then swims for 1 point.

Swim points

Purple → Blue AnyTime swim5
Dock swim4
PASS swim (3 PASS cards)3
Blue AM/PM swim2
Season corner recovery swim1

Winning

The game ends when all Time cards have been drawn (or, if you choose, when someone reaches the target score). Highest total wins. Tie breaker: each tied player draws one more Time card; the card closest to the next available time on the board wins.

Digital table choices

  • The Time deck is 28 cards: four colors × 9 AM–3 PM, including a NOON in each color.
  • PASS cards may be any colors. Ducks start on the lake shore and return to the lake after every swim.
  • A single Green, Orange or Blue NOON moves your duck to a Purple AnyTime space; if your next card is not a NOON, a Green or Orange NOON is discarded (the Blue NOON stays in hand as a Blue card).
  • Holding the Blue NOON counts as your first NOON when you draw another non-Yellow NOON.
  • While waiting in a corner, a card that doesn't free you is discarded.
  • Tie breaker: the "next available time" is the earliest time (9 AM onward) with an empty space on the board; closest card wins.
