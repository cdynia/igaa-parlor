Finish the Appointment

How to play · Game #261

The goal

Finish the most appointments. An appointment is three matching cards of the same season color: one Season/Month, one Day and one Time.

Setup

Shuffle all the cards and place the deck face down in the center of the mat, with a space for each season around it. The player left of the dealer goes first.

Your turn

  • A card is playable if its season space holds no card of that type yet (Season/Month, Day or Time). Each space holds one of each type, in any order.
  • If any card in your four season piles is playable, you must play one (tap it). You don't draw that turn.
  • With no playable cards, draw one card. If it's playable, it's played immediately. If not, it goes into your matching season pile.
  • When a season shows two types and you place the third, you finish the appointment. You take all three cards, and that season starts over for the next player.

Strategy

Hold out on your Season/Month cards when you have a choice. They're the most important and hardest to get, and only three exist per season. Try not to leave two types showing for the next player to finish!

End of the game

Once all 3 months of a season are finished, that season is complete: any leftover cards of that season are stacked face down. When all 12 appointments are made, the player with the most wins. Tied players share the win.
