Beginner Game · 2–8 players

I've Got an Appointment

Collect a season, a day, and a time — all the same color — to book your appointment first. Draw a card, discard a card, and race the table.

How many opponents?

A full deck is 68 color-coded cards: four seasons (Winter blue · Spring gold · Summer green · Fall pumpkin), each with 3 months, 7 days, and 7 times.

How to play

Book the appointment first

Winter Spring Summer Fall

The goal

Be the first to hold a 3-card appointment: one season/month card, one day-of-week card, and one time card — all of the same color. Then declare it.

Your turn

  • Draw one card — either the top of the draw pile (face down) or the top of the discard pile (face up).
  • Discard one card from your hand, so you're back to five.
  • If your five cards ever contain a matching-color season + day + time, the Declare appointment button lights up. Click it to win.

Strategy

Season/month cards are the scarcest — only three per color. When you land one, build the rest of your appointment around its color and hold onto it.
