Center Stage

Spin the color spinner and move around the board. Collect cards to make Rainbow appointments — a Season/Month, a Day and a Time in three different colors. Land in the Center Stage and every discard comes to you. Most Rainbows wins!

Players (you + computer)
Computer pace
How to play · Game #250

Center Stage

Object

Make the most Rainbow appointments: one Season/Month card, one Day card and one Time card, all in different colors. Entering the Center Stage is exciting because you collect every discard while you are there — sometimes many, sometimes only a few before you are bumped out.

Setup

Each pawn starts on its lettered corner: Y Yellow/Spring, G Green/Summer, O Orange/Fall, B Blue/Winter. One card starts the discard pile in the Center Stage. Your cards are sorted face up into Season/Month, Day and Time piles.

Your turn

  • First turn: spin and move to the corner of the color spun.
  • After that: spin and move clockwise to the next space of that color.
  • Draw: if the Center Stage is empty you may take the top discard when it matches the spin color. Otherwise draw from the deck — keep it only if it matches the spin; if not, it is discarded into the Center Stage.
  • Same color as your space? You enter the Center Stage.
  • Next space taken? Send that player to the Center Stage, take their space, draw a card, and end your turn.

On the Center Stage

Only one player at a time. You don't spin, move or draw — you collect every card the others discard. When you can make a Rainbow appointment, you make it on your turn, return to your own corner and draw a card.

Winning

Play until all twelve appointments are made (there are 12 Season/Month cards) or no more can be made. Most Rainbow appointments wins.

Digital table choices

  • Spinning your own space's color while someone is on stage bumps them back to their own corner (they keep their cards) and you take the stage.
  • A pawn bumped onto the stage replaces any performer, who returns to their own corner.
  • You may take a matching discard on any turn while the stage is empty. Entering the stage ends your turn without a draw.
  • Rainbow appointments are booked automatically, as many as your cards allow. The game ends when the deck runs out or all 12 are made.
