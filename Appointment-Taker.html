Appointment Taker

The twelve Season/Month cards wait on the mat. Use your three Day and Time cards to build appointments, upgrade them, and TAKE the best ones, even from other players. Only a 5-point appointment is safe.

How to play · Game #065

Take the best appointments

Setup

The 12 Season/Month cards sit on the mat in four stacks: Spring (March on top), Summer (June), Fall (September), Winter (December). Players get only Day and Time cards, three each. The player left of the dealer goes first.

An appointment

The top Season/Month card of a stack plus one Day and one Time. The Day and Time can come from your hand or from that season's Day/Time spot on the mat. You may use one, two, or all three of your cards, and make more than one appointment in a turn.

On your turn you may

  • Make an appointment: select your cards, then tap a glowing Season stack.
  • Upgrade any appointment: replace one card to raise its points. You keep the replaced card. Upgrade another player's appointment and you TAKE it.
  • Rainbow → Original: the only upgrade that may replace two cards, and it must reach 3+ points.
  • Swap: use a Day/Time card from the mat somewhere else, only if it immediately makes an appointment or upgrade. Then put one of your cards on an empty mat spot.
  • Can't do any of that? Place one card on an empty mat spot (Days on top, Times on the bottom). No legal spot means you pass.
  • End your turn and draw back up to 3 cards.

Appointment types

5ORIGINAL + WEEKEND + NOONProtected: can never be taken.
4ORIGINAL + WEEKEND + any timeUpgrade: add a NOON.
4ORIGINAL + WED + NOONUpgrade: replace WED with a matching WEEKEND.
3ORIGINAL + WED or NOONA NOON appointment jumps to 5 with a matching WEEKEND.
2ORIGINAL or RAINBOWOriginal = all 3 cards the same season color. Rainbow = 3 different colors.
1WHATEVERAny other color combo of Season/Month + Day + Time.

End of the round

Take what you can before the last Season/Month card leaves the mat! After that, no new appointments and no taking. Everyone gets one last chance to upgrade their own appointments with the cards in hand, and no new cards are dealt. Score your appointments. The most points wins the round, and the first player to reach the target (100) wins the game.
