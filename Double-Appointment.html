How to play · Game #277

Double Appointment

Object

Complete your appointment by collecting your Season/Month card and the matching 2 Day and 2 Time cards. Players place cards into each other's INBOX to advance their own appointment while blocking others. Every turn is a choice between helping yourself or forcing another player to pass — and the game can suddenly end in a LOCKBOX.

Setup

Each player picks a Birth Month Season/Month card; add 2 Day cards and 2 Time cards of that season's color for each player (20 cards for 4 players, 10 for 2). Deal 5 cards face up to each player so everyone can see them.

Your turn

  • Place one card into another player's INBOX. Only one card can be in any INBOX at a time.
  • You may take the card from your INBOX only if you can place a card into another INBOX that same turn. It replaces one of your face-up cards.
  • If you cannot place a card, you must pass until you can.
  • Everyone can see how many cards each player still needs.

Winning

The first player to replace all five starting cards with their Double Appointment wins. If every player passes in sequence and no one can play, call "LOCKBOX!" — the game ends with no winner.

Strategy

Being dealt one of your own cards helps, but others can see what you need — and may block your INBOX or force you into a card you didn't intend to play.

Digital table choices

  • Your 2 Day and 2 Time cards are chosen at random from your color. Each player's birth month comes from a different season, so your color is your appointment (green check = one of yours).
  • Any card may go into any empty INBOX, including a card you just took.
  • If you place a card without taking one, you are down to four cards; your next turn is spent taking your INBOX card (or passing if it is empty). This keeps everyone able to reach five.
  • A LOCKBOX is called when every player passes in a row.
