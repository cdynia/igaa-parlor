How to play · Game #114

Be first to each appointment

The goal

An appointment is a set of one Season/Month, one Day and one Time card. There are 12 appointment types on the mat, and only one player can make each type. Hold the most points when the round ends.

Setup

Deal 7 cards to each player. Everyone lays their cards face up in three rows by type (Season/Month, Day, Time) for all to see. The player left of the dealer goes first.

Your turn

  • Draw the top card of the deck into your rows.
  • If you can make an open appointment type, select its three cards and tap the glowing rung. The cards move in front of you and your token goes on that number.
  • Upgrade: only you can upgrade your own appointment. Swap cards from your rows into it to make a higher, open type. Your token moves up, the old type is released for anyone, and the swapped-out cards return to your rows.
  • One appointment or upgrade per turn, then play passes left.

The 12 types

12TODAY · today's month + today's day of the week
11BIRTHDAY · your birth month, all the same color (ORIGINAL)
10BIRTHDAY · your birth month, NON-ORIGINAL
9HUMP DAY & HIGH NOON · Wednesday + 12:00 Noon, ORIGINAL
8HUMP DAY & HIGH NOON · NON-ORIGINAL
7WEEKEND & HIGH NOON · Saturday/Sunday + Noon, ORIGINAL
6WEEKEND & HIGH NOON · NON-ORIGINAL
5WEEKEND · Saturday/Sunday, ORIGINAL
4WEEKEND · NON-ORIGINAL
3ORIGINAL · all three the same season
2RAINBOW · three different seasons
1WHATEVER · two of the same season

End of the round

The round ends when all 12 appointments are made or the deck runs out. Score the numbers your tokens sit on. If all 12 are made, points are doubled (very rare!). The most points wins the round; keep dealing rounds until someone reaches the target.
