How to play · Level 1 Beginner board

Level 1 Seasons

Object

Make an appointment: a Time card, a Day card and a Season/Month card. When it's done, it's set aside. Keep trading to help the other players finish theirs. The game ends when every player has made an appointment.

Setup

Each pawn starts on the UP ARROW of its season. The player next to the dealer goes first.

Moving

Spin and move that many spaces — up your season, around the Trade Circle, and back down your season, counting your season name space. At the top of your section move one space right, then up into the Trade Circle; go clockwise around it, then back down and right into your section.

Cards

  • Start of your turn: if the top discard matches the color you're on, you may take it — and your turn ends.
  • After you move: if the top discard matches the color you land on, take it or draw from the deck. A drawn card that matches is kept; if not, discard it. Your turn ends.
  • Spin a zero: draw a card and keep it. Same color as your space? Stay. Different color? Jump to the next space of that color.
  • Pass your season space: take a bonus card from the deck or the discard pile.
  • Trade Circle: when you're in it and have a card to trade, you may ask for a card that matches the color of your space. No two players share a Trade Circle space — skip to the next open one.

Color Chart

Advanced appointments

Original (all one color) · Rainbow (all different colors) · Whatever (any mix) · Birthday (your birth month). Everyone must make a different type — the first to finish claims theirs and the rest choose from what's left. Nobody may use another player's birth-month card.

Race to the finish line

When everyone has an appointment, the first player back to their own season space wins!

Digital table choices

  • The spinner shows 0, 1, 2 and 3. The UP arrow, season name and DOWN arrow are spaces in your season's color.
  • The Trade Circle has 8 spaces — two halves of each color. You enter above your own color and ride all the way around.
  • A trade: pick a player with a card of your space's color; they give you one and you give back any card you choose.
  • Appointments are booked automatically as soon as your cards make one.
